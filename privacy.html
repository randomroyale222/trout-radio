Trout Radio privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who uses it

Trout Radio is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.

What it does with Google and YouTube data

The owner signs in with their own YouTube account. Trout Radio then uses the YouTube Data API for two things only:

  • reading the entries of the owner's stream playlist, and
  • removing an entry from that playlist when the owner chooses to remove a song.

It does not upload, edit or delete videos, does not post or comment, does not change channel settings and does not access any other playlist.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in is stored only on the owner's computer. Trout Radio does not collect data about anyone else, does not use analytics or cookies, and does not sell, share or transfer any data. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
