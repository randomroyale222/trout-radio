🐟 Trout Radio

The stream's music. These work in chat on both Twitch and Kick.

Request songs

!sr song name
Request a song by name and artist (subs & mods).
!wrongsong
Take back your last request.
!queue
See what's coming up.

What's playing

!song
The song playing now.
!lastsong
The song before this one.

Chat power

!voteskip
Vote to skip the current song. Enough votes within 30 seconds and it's gone.
!curators
Top curators: viewers whose requests got kept in the stream playlist.
!mysongs
Which of your requests made it into the playlist.

Channel points

Priority Song Request
Your song jumps the queue. Works for everyone, not just subs.
Skip Song
Skip the current song right away.

Mods

!skip
Skip the current song.
!keep
Add the current song to the stream playlist (and credit whoever requested it).
!vol 0-100
Set the music volume.

Tip: request good songs. If the streamer keeps yours, you climb the curator leaderboard.
